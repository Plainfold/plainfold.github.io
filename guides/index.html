Guides for a big move.

Moving country is a chain of appointments and forms, and each one unlocks the next. Our guides put them in the right order, explain them in plain English and link every rule to the official source. The first one covers your first 90 days in Spain.

Spain Arrival Kit

Your first 90 days in Spain, in the order that saves the most time.

A calm, step-by-step guide to the NIE, padrón, TIE card, bank account, Social Security, autónomo registration, the Beckham regime and healthcare, for people moving to Spain on a digital nomad, non-lucrative or student visa.

Written for Americans, Brits and other non-EU nationals arriving on the digital nomad visa, the non-lucrative visa or a student stay. It isn’t for EU citizens, and it isn’t a visa application guide: it starts once you have your visa.
